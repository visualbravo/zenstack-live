## Replication Management

Zebezium reads through a replication slot, and Postgres keeps WAL until the slot has read it. If Zebezium stays down, WAL builds up until the disk is full.

Monitor slot lag with `pg_replication_slots`, and consider `max_slot_wal_keep_size`, which is available from Postgres 13.

It caps the WAL, at the cost of Zebezium losing its place if the cap is hit.
